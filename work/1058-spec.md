# 1058: auto-decision-log / verification.md: test-gate entries can under-enumerate failure causes with no signal of incompleteness

Surface: backend

Origin: reflect full (from #994 wrap-up, Near-misses lens, chain-walked to systemic)
Defer-reason: tangential

## Current State

`_shared/auto-decision-log.md`'s canonical entry schema and `test/verification.md`'s reporting guidance do not require a test-gate decision-log entry that names specific failure causes to account for every failure in a stated count.

Observed on #994's wrap-up: the first of that group's two `/flow` calls wrote a `decisions.md` Step-4 test-gate entry stating "8 fail" and naming 6 of them by category (chmod-based permission tests, installed-plugin version mismatch, two remote-dependent reconcile tests) — silently omitting the other 2 (`impeccable-plugin-contract.test.js`'s `gatherSignals()`/CLI-entrypoint failures, "could not resolve an installed root at the pin" — environmental, Impeccable not installed in that sandbox). The omission was invisible to a reader trusting the entry; it surfaced only because the second call independently re-ran the full suite from raw output instead of trusting the prior claim, per that dispatch's own explicit "re-derive from artifacts, never a prior claim" instruction.

Why was this possible? The entry's author enumerated known/recognized failure categories from experience with the suite rather than tallying named causes against the log's actual `not ok` count. Why did that go unnoticed? Neither schema requires a partial-enumeration summary to account for 100% of a stated count, or to fall back to "N failures, see full log" when it can't. Reproducible on any future decisions.md entry anywhere in the plugin that summarizes a multi-cause test-gate result this way — systemic, not a one-off.

## Deliverables

1. Add a line to `_shared/auto-decision-log.md`'s canonical entry schema (or `test/verification.md`'s reporting guidance, whichever more directly governs test-gate entries) requiring: a decision-log entry that names specific failure causes must account for every failure in the stated count, or state the count with "see full log at {path}" instead of a partial named list that could be mistaken for complete.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A future decisions.md test-gate entry either enumerates every failure by name/cause, or explicitly signals the enumeration is partial / points to the full log — never a bare partial list with no signal of incompleteness.
