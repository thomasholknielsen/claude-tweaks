## Current State

`plugin/skills/assess-agent-autonomy/grant-check.md` Step 1 gathers a record's body and labels and Step 2 judges `RECOMMEND_BUILD` / `RECOMMEND_MERGE`. `plugin/skills/backlog/grant-mode.md` invokes it inline (`Skill(skill: "claude-tweaks:assess-agent-autonomy", args: "grant-check #{n}")`) and runs headlessly under the live `.claude-tweaks/routines/claude-tweaks-backlog-grant-weekdays.yml` routine. Neither side carries an untrusted-content stance: the fetched body is passed as bare prose and the verdict line is read with no source constraint.

Exposure is narrower than `ceremony-check`'s (#1274): only `by:*` machine-filed records reach `grant-check` — `grant-mode.md` refuses human-filed records unconditionally — so a drive-by issue author cannot reach it without also being able to apply a `by:*` label. But the verdict is gating in the strongest sense the plugin has: it grants `auto:build` / `auto:merge`, which `/dispatch` then acts on without a human.

The two-sided contract this record consumes — caller wraps in the collision-resistant `BEGIN`/`END UNTRUSTED RECORD CONTENT` markers and reads the verdict only from the callee's own rendered output, never defaulting on a missing verdict line; callee treats wrapped content as untrusted regardless of call site — is `plugin/skills/_shared/untrusted-record-content.md`, created by #1275 and extended to `ceremony-check` by #1274. This record is the third extension, named as a follow-up in that design (2026-08-24) rather than folded in, to keep #1274's blast radius at the two judgments sharing one call site.
