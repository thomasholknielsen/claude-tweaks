## Current State

`plugin/skills/backlog/refine-mode.md` Step 3 invokes `Skill(skill: "claude-tweaks:assess-agent-autonomy", args: "grant-check #{n}")` once per worklist record — `grant-check.md`'s own header names it as a caller of "the identical call" `grant-mode.md` makes. #1391 extended `_shared/untrusted-record-content.md` to `grant-mode.md` (caller side: wrap the record's title + body per the contract; read `^RECOMMEND_BUILD: (true|false)$` / `^RECOMMEND_MERGE: (true|false)$` only from the mode's own rendered output; a missing verdict line is a per-candidate skip with `failedKey: 'grant-check-no-verdict'`) and to `grant-check.md` Step 1 (callee side, "regardless of which call site supplied it"). `refine-mode.md` got neither: it passes the body unwrapped and its `RECOMMEND_BUILD: true`/`false` branching has no missing-verdict case, so a rendered output with no verdict line has no defined outcome there.

Exposure is lower than `grant-mode.md`'s — `refine`'s Grant lane is a recommendation a human batch-confirms before any label is written — and the callee-side obligation already applies to this call. But the contract's Scope says "wrap on every entry path — interactive or headless", so the caller side is out of contract at a live call site.

The blocker is size: `refine-mode.md` is 40,933 B against the 40,960 B ceiling (`tests/bin-lib/skill-audit/context-cost.test.js`), and it is pinned by several suites (`tests/backlog-refine-foldin-no-truncation.test.js`, `tests/grant-mode-inprogress-pin.test.js`, others). Adding a paragraph needs a deliberate slim-first pass, not a squeeze — the same shape #1274 hit with `record-creation.md` and deferred.
